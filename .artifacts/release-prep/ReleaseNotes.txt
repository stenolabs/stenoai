Steno 0.8.1 lets you edit generated notes, share them from one menu, and choose an additional speaker detection engine on macOS.

### Notes and sharing
- Edit generated sections of a saved note. Steno keeps the earlier model output available and asks before leaving with unsaved edits.
- Copy or export notes and transcripts from the Share menu. On macOS, send a note through the system share sheet.
- Custom reports with flat YAML frontmatter show those fields in a folded Properties panel and in PDF exports.

### Recording and models
- On macOS, Nemotron 3 is an optional speaker detection engine. Its models download before switching; Sortformer remains the default.
- Italian is selectable in transcription settings, and Auto can detect Italian transcripts.
- Recording can continue with system audio when no microphone is present. Paused-recording notifications remain until you act, and local requests keep working while offline.
- Re-transcription now includes every tracked segment of a continued note. OpenAI-compatible providers can return text in typed chunks without breaking summaries.

### App and website
- The desktop app is named Steno again. The website has expanded government, defence, and law-enforcement pages, updated navigation, and an app film.

### Upgrade notes
- The app ID, `stenoai://` links, and meeting/config storage stay compatible. On first launch of the packaged app, v0.8.0 chat files are copied into the current app data directory if they are absent.
- Continued notes created before segment tracking cannot be safely re-transcribed. Steno leaves them unchanged and explains why.
- The macOS package targets Apple Silicon on macOS 14.4 or later.
- The Traditional Chinese catalogue is present, but locale selection is not yet available and the interface remains English. Windows and Linux remain alpha platforms with unsigned installers.
