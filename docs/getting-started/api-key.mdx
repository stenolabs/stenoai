Steno writes notes and answers chat with a local model by default. To use a faster cloud model instead, bring your own API key from OpenRouter, OpenAI or Anthropic. Your audio still never leaves your computer.

## Choose a provider

| Provider | Good for |
|----------|----------|
| **OpenRouter** | The easiest start. One key gives you many models from different companies. |
| **OpenAI** | GPT models, from the makers of ChatGPT. |
| **Anthropic** | Claude models. |

AWS Bedrock and custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints are also supported. Set them up in **Settings → AI**; see [Summarization models](/models/summarization-models#using-a-cloud-model).

## Create a key

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="OpenRouter">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Sign in or create an account at [openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add credit">
        Add a few dollars of credit to your account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a key">
        Open [Keys](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys), create a key, and copy it. It starts with `sk-or-`.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="OpenAI">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Sign in to the [OpenAI platform](https://platform.openai.com). This is separate from a ChatGPT subscription.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add credit">
        Add credit under **Billing**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a key">
        Open [API keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys), create a secret key, and copy it. It starts with `sk-`.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Anthropic">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Sign in to the [Claude Console](https://console.anthropic.com). This is separate from a Claude subscription.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add credit">
        Add credit under **Billing**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a key">
        Open [API keys](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys), create a key, and copy it. It starts with `sk-ant-`.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
Treat your key like a password. Don't share it or paste it anywhere except Steno.
</Warning>

## Add the key to Steno

**During setup**: on **How should Steno think?**, choose **Cloud**, pick your provider, and paste the key in **Paste your API key**. Click **Connect**.

**Later**: open **Settings → AI**, set **AI provider** to **Cloud API**, choose the **Service**, and paste the key in **API key**. Click **Test connection**.

During setup, Steno recognises the key from its prefix and picks the matching provider. It then checks the key with the provider. When it works you'll see **Connected · *model* ready**. If the key is rejected or the account has no credit, Steno says so; add credit or create a new key and try again.

The key is stored encrypted on this device and is only ever sent to the provider you choose.

## What it costs

You pay the provider directly, based on how much text you send and receive. Steno adds no charge.

A meeting's notes or a chat answer is usually a small amount of text, so a few dollars of credit typically goes a long way. Costs vary a lot by model, and longer meetings cost more. Check current prices before you choose a model:

- [OpenRouter models and pricing](https://openrouter.ai/models)
- [OpenAI pricing](https://openai.com/api/pricing/)
- [Anthropic pricing](https://www.anthropic.com/pricing#api)

Most providers let you set a spending limit on the key or account.

## What is sent to the provider

With a cloud provider, Steno sends text, never audio:

- **Notes**: the transcript and any notes you typed.
- **Chat**: your question and the meeting context you selected.
- **Suggested actions**: the note's title, summary and action items.

Recording and transcription still run on your computer.

<Note>
Settings also offers a separate cloud speech-to-text engine. That one does send audio, and Steno warns you before you turn it on. Choosing a cloud AI provider does not turn it on.
</Note>

## Switch back to local

Open **Settings → AI** and set **AI provider** to **Local (on-device)**, then select a model. If the model isn't downloaded yet, Steno downloads it. Local models are private and free, and need no internet connection once downloaded.
