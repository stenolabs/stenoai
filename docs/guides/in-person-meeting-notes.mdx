---
title: "How to take notes in an in-person meeting on a Mac"
description: "Record and summarize in-person meetings on macOS with Steno. Your Mac's microphone captures the room; transcription and notes are generated on-device."
---

To take notes in an in-person meeting on a Mac, use Steno with your microphone: it records the room through your Mac's mic, then transcribes on your device and summarizes locally by default. There is no meeting app and no bot involved — just the microphone — so it works for any face-to-face discussion.

## Steps

1. Install Steno and grant microphone permission (see [Installation](/getting-started/installation)).
2. In Steno, make sure the **Record system audio** toggle is off (microphone recording is always on), then click **New note** to start.
3. Place your Mac where its mic can hear the room clearly.
4. When the meeting ends, click **Stop**; Steno transcribes the recording and lets you generate notes when you are ready, or you can enable automatic notes under **Settings → AI**.

## Speaker labels in person

An in-person meeting is captured on the microphone only, so there is no separate `[Others]` system-audio channel.
On macOS, Steno can still separate up to four voices within that microphone stream after processing.
The main voice keeps `[You]`, while additional voices receive labels such as `Speaker 2` and `Speaker 3`.
Review the groups in the note's **Speakers** section before relying on them.
See [Speaker labels](/features/speaker-labels) for naming, privacy, and platform details.

## Getting a clean transcript

Room audio can be noisier than a call. For in-person recordings where accuracy matters, the [Whisper engine](/models/transcription-models) can be a good choice. Position the Mac close to the speakers and minimize background noise where you can.

<Accordion title="Can Steno tell individual people apart in the room?">
  Yes, on macOS.

Anonymous speaker separation works without named voice profiles.

It can distinguish up to four voices in the microphone channel.

Review the result because short, overlapping, or noisy speech can be grouped incorrectly.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the recording uploaded anywhere?">
  No.

The audio recording stays on your Mac.
Transcription runs locally, but the transcript can be sent off-device through the optional features described below.

Summarization runs on your Mac by default.

If you configure a cloud model, private server, or organisation adapter, Steno sends the transcript and any notes you typed off-device for summarization, but never the audio.

Configured organisation sharing features can also send transcripts and notes to your organisation.

Steno sends anonymous usage analytics by default, without meeting content.

Turn analytics off in **Settings → Advanced**.

See [How on-device processing works](/privacy/how-on-device-works).

</Accordion>
