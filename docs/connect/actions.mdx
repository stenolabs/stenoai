Actions turn a meeting's action items into work in the apps you use. When a note is ready, Steno suggests follow-ups such as a Linear issue or an email draft. You review each one, edit it if needed, and approve it. Nothing runs until you do, unless you have chosen **Always allow** for that app (see [Approve an app once](#approve-an-app-once)).

## Connect an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connect">
    Click **Connect** in the sidebar and scroll to **Actions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an app">
    Click **Connect** on the app's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Your browser opens the app's sign-in page. Approve access, then return to Steno. Steno never sees your password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| App | What Steno can do | Status |
|-----|-------------------|--------|
| **Linear** | Create issues from action items. | Available |
| **Notion** | Add pages and tasks to your workspace. | Available |
| **Jira and Confluence** | Create Jira tickets and Confluence pages. Your Atlassian admin must allow Rovo MCP. | Available |
| **Email** | Draft follow-up emails in your mail app. Built in, no sign-in needed. | Available |
| **Custom MCP server** | Any MCP server by URL or a command on this computer, including n8n, Zapier and Make. See [Custom MCP servers](/connect/custom-mcp-servers). | Available |
| **Slack** | Post meeting summaries to channels. | Coming soon |
| **HubSpot** | Log meetings and follow-ups to your CRM. | Coming soon |
| **Asana** | Turn action items into tasks. | Coming soon |

<Tip>
Until Slack is available, you can post to Slack through an n8n or Zapier workflow added as a [custom MCP server](/connect/custom-mcp-servers).
</Tip>

Your connected apps appear in **Settings → Connectors** under **Your connections**. Each card shows where the app runs, how many actions it offers, a **Run actions without asking** switch, and buttons to **Refresh**, **Sign in** again or **Disconnect**. Use the switch at the top of a card to stop using that app for suggestions without disconnecting it.

## How suggestions work

<Steps>
  <Step title="A note is ready">
    When Steno finishes a note, it reads the note's title, summary and action items and proposes up to five follow-ups. It uses the AI model you set in **Settings → AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the cards">
    Cards appear under the note in **Suggested actions**. Each card says what it will do and where, for example "Create issue in Linear", and which action item it came from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit if needed">
    Change any field on the card before you approve it. Required fields are marked. For a tool whose fields cannot be edited on the card, the card lists what will be sent instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or dismiss">
    Click **Approve** to send that action, exactly as shown, and nothing else. Click **Always allow for &lt;app&gt;** to send it and let that app's future actions run without asking. Click **Dismiss** to discard it; nothing is sent. Once an action is done, the card shows **Done** with an **Open** link to the new item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To suggest again, or to get suggestions for an older note, click **Suggest actions** in the note.

To stop automatic suggestions, turn off **Suggest actions after each note** in **Settings → Connectors**. You can still click **Suggest actions** whenever you want them.

**Recent actions** in **Settings → Connectors** lists what you have approved and whether it succeeded. Actions that ran because you chose **Always allow** are marked **ran automatically**.

## Approve an app once

You do not have to approve every action from an app you trust. On any card, click **Always allow for &lt;app&gt;** instead of **Approve**. That action runs, and from then on Steno runs that app's actions as soon as it suggests them: the card appears already marked **Done**, with an **Open** link, and chat actions on that app run without pausing.

Steno still asks you first when:

- a suggested action has a missing or invalid field, so you can fix it;
- the connection is turned off or your organisation does not allow it;
- the tool is one the app marks as destructive (these are never suggested at all).

To turn it off, open **Settings → Connectors** and switch off **Run actions without asking** on that app's card. For email drafts, the switch is on the **Email** card on the Connect page. The next action from that app asks for approval again.

Every action is still recorded in **Recent actions**, whether you approved it or it ran automatically.

## Email drafts

The **Email** action drafts a follow-up email in your own mail app.

- The draft opens in your default mail app. Nothing is sent until you press **Send** there.
- Recipients are only taken from email addresses written in the note. Steno does not guess or look up addresses. If the note has none, the recipient field is left empty.
- If the message is too long to open directly, Steno puts the full text on your clipboard so you can paste it in.

To stop email suggestions, turn off **Email** on the Connect page.

## Privacy

- **Suggesting** uses only the note's title, summary and action items. Your transcript and your own typed notes are never used. If your AI model is a cloud provider, that text is sent to the provider.
- **Approving** sends the card's contents to that app, and nothing else.
- Every connection is labelled with where it runs: **Runs on this computer**, **On your local network** or **On the internet**. Each card also says where it sends data, for example "Leaves your device for Linear."
- Sign-in tokens and secrets are kept in your system's secure storage.

## Organisation policy

If you are signed in to an organisation, your admin can turn actions off, limit which apps and servers you can connect, block commands on this computer, turn off email drafts, or require approval for every action (`mcp_auto_approve_allowed: false`, which hides **Always allow** and the **Run actions without asking** switch). Steno then shows a message such as "Your organisation has turned off actions." or marks a connection **Not allowed by your organisation**.

If Steno cannot reach your organisation's settings, it keeps using the last policy it received for up to 24 hours. After that, actions are paused until Steno can reach them again.

<Accordion title="Can Steno take actions from chat too?">
Yes. With a capable AI model, chat can use your connected apps and asks you to approve each change, unless you chose **Always allow** for that app. See [Chat](/features/ask-your-meetings#take-actions-in-connected-apps).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which model writes the suggestions?">
The AI model set in **Settings → AI**. With the default local model, suggesting runs on your device.
</Accordion>
