---
title: "Instant transcripts"
description: "With Parakeet, your transcript and a first summary are ready the moment you press Stop. Steno then refines both in the background."
icon: "forward-fast"
---

With the **Parakeet** engine, you don't wait for transcription after a meeting. When you press **Stop**, Steno saves the live transcript straight away, writes a first summary from it, and then refines both in the background.

## What happens after Stop

<Steps>
  <Step title="The live transcript is saved">
    The transcript you saw while recording becomes the meeting's transcript immediately, with timestamps. When both sides of a call were recorded, turns are labelled **You** and **Others**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An early summary appears">
    Steno writes notes from the live transcript, so you can read the summary and action items within moments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Steno refines the transcript">
    In the background, Steno transcribes the full recording again for better accuracy and speaker labels. The note shows **Refining transcript** and "Speaker labels and notes update here when it's done."
  </Step>
  <Step title="The final note replaces the early one">
    When refining finishes, the transcript, speaker labels and summary update in place. If you renamed the meeting in the meantime, your title is kept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While Steno is refining, the note is read-only. Any edits you start are saved once it finishes. See [Editing notes](/features/editing-notes#when-a-note-is-read-only).

If the refined summary can't be generated, Steno keeps the early summary and offers **Generate notes** so you can try again.

## When there's no early summary

Steno skips the early summary and writes notes once, after refining, when:

- you continued recording on an existing note;
- the early summary fails for any reason.

If **Generate notes automatically** is turned off in **Settings → AI**, Steno doesn't write notes on its own at all. Click **Generate notes** when you want them.

## With Whisper

The **Whisper** engine has no live transcript, so there is nothing to save instantly. After you press **Stop**, Steno transcribes the recording and then writes the notes. Switch engines in **Settings → Transcribe**; see [Transcription models](/models/transcription-models).

---

<Accordion title="Can I turn instant transcripts off?">
There is no separate setting. Instant transcripts come with the Parakeet engine. To wait for the full transcript before notes are written, switch to Whisper.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does refining send anything off my device?">
No. Refining runs the same on-device transcription as the rest of Steno. If you use a cloud AI provider, the transcript text is sent to it for the summary, as usual.
</Accordion>
