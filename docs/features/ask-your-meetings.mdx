Chat answers questions about your meetings using your configured AI provider. Open **Chat** in the sidebar to ask across your notes, or use the ask bar at the bottom of a note to ask about that meeting.

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open chat">
    Click **Chat** in the sidebar, or click the ask bar at the bottom of an open note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the context">
    Use the **Context** menu to choose which meetings the question is about. See below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    Type your question and press Return. Follow-up questions keep the recent conversation in mind.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Past chats are listed under **Recents** on the Chat page.

## Choose the context

The **Context** menu controls which meeting information goes with your question:

| Context | What chat uses |
|---------|----------------|
| **This meeting** | The open note: summary, topics, key points, action items, your notes and the transcript. While recording, the speech transcribed so far. Available in a note's ask bar. |
| **All notes** | The default. A selection of your recent saved notes. Very old notes may be left out, so open a note to ask about it directly. |
| A space | Notes in that space only. |
| **Shared notes** | Your organisation's shared notes. Shown only when you're signed in to an organisation. |
| **No context** | Nothing from your meetings. Use it for general questions. |

Answers should separate general knowledge from what was said in your meetings. If there isn't enough evidence in your notes, chat should say what's missing.

## During recording

You can chat as soon as recording starts. Keep **This meeting** selected to ask about what has been said so far.

With Parakeet, transcribed speech is added as the meeting goes on; new speech is available to your next question. Whisper transcribes after you stop, so during a Whisper recording chat has no live transcript to read.

Recording continues while you chat, and the conversation stays attached to the saved note.

## See what chat is doing

While chat works, it shows **Thinking** with a timer, and a line for each step it takes, such as "Read 12 notes", "Searched your notes" or "Searched Linear". The steps stay visible above the answer once it arrives, so you can see what it was based on.

## Use a recipe

On the **Chat** page, type `/` at the start of the message box to open your recipes, such as **Write weekly recap** or **Decision log**. Keep typing to filter, use the arrow keys to choose, and press Return or Tab. The recipe fills the message box; edit it if you like, then send.

See [Cookbooks and recipes](/features/cookbooks) to browse recipes or write your own.

## Charts

Ask for a chart and chat draws one in the answer, for example "Chart how many meetings I had with each customer this month".

- **Types**: bar (including stacked and horizontal), line, area, pie, donut, scatter, radar, and table.
- **From your notes**: chat only charts numbers that appear in your notes or can be counted from them. It doesn't invent values or fill gaps with zeros.
- **From your own numbers**: numbers you give in the chat are charted as given.
- **General knowledge**: for questions not about your notes, chat may chart widely known public figures. It says in the title or text that these values are approximate and the period they cover.

Under each chart, click **View data** to see the numbers, **Copy data** to copy them as CSV, or **Copy as image**.

## Take actions in connected apps

If you have [connected apps](/connect/actions), you can ask chat to act on them, for example "Create a Linear issue for the pricing follow-up".

- Chat can look things up in a connected app without asking, when the app marks the tool as read-only.
- Anything that creates or changes something appears as a card. Review or edit it, then click **Approve** to send it, or **Dismiss**. Nothing that writes runs without your approval.
- To stop approving every change from an app you trust, click **Always allow for &lt;app&gt;** on its card. Later changes to that app run as soon as chat asks for them and show as a **Done** line in the reply. Turn it off in **Settings → Connectors** with **Run actions without asking**. See [Approve an app once](/connect/actions#approve-an-app-once).

Taking actions needs a model that can use tools reliably:

- **Cloud providers**: OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS Bedrock, OpenRouter and custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints.
- **Local or Private Server models**: Qwen 3 and Qwen 3.5, gpt-oss, Gemma 4 12B and 27B, Llama 3.1 and 3.3, and Mistral Small.

Other models, including the default Gemma 4 E2B, answer in plain text. If you ask such a model to act, chat shows a notice that the model can't use your connected apps, with a link to **Open AI settings**. Organisation-managed AI also answers in plain text.

## What you can ask

- "What decisions were made?"
- "Who is responsible for the API migration?"
- "What did I promise people this week?"
- "Summarize this in three bullet points."
- "Chart the action items per person from last week's meetings."

Specific questions work better than vague ones.

## Privacy

Chat follows your **AI provider** setting. With a local model, questions and meeting content stay on your device. A Private Server, cloud or organisation provider receives your question and the context you selected, and any notes chat reads while answering. **No context** sends no meeting content.

Approved actions are sent to the app you approved them for. See [Actions → Privacy](/connect/actions#privacy).

---

<Accordion title="The answers are not very accurate -- what should I try?">
Larger models produce better answers. In **Settings → AI**, switch to `qwen3.5:9b` or `gemma4:12b-it-qat`, which can also take actions. Also check that the full transcript was saved -- if transcription failed, the model only has the summary to work from.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I ask in a language other than English?">
Yes. Chat passes your question to the language model, which supports many languages. Results vary by model and language.
</Accordion>
