---
title: "Ask your meetings"
description: "Ask about a live meeting, saved notes, or general topics using your configured AI provider."
---

Use the ask bar while recording or when viewing a saved note. Questions use your configured AI provider, and follow-up questions include recent conversation history for the selected context.

## How to use it

1. Open a saved note from the sidebar
2. Click the **Ask** bar at the bottom of the pane
3. Type your question and press Return

General questions work with any context, even before anyone speaks or when a folder is empty. The **Context** menu controls which meeting information accompanies your question:

- **This meeting** uses the saved note, or a snapshot of finalized speech during recording.
- **All notes** or a folder uses a bounded selection of saved meeting summaries.
- **No meeting context** answers without attaching meeting content or conversation turns from another context.
- **Shared notes**, when signed in, queries your organisation's shared notes.

Answers should distinguish general knowledge from what was said in the meeting. When you ask about a meeting without enough evidence, the assistant should say what is missing.

## During recording

You can chat as soon as recording starts, including with Whisper or before speech has been transcribed. Keep **Context: This meeting** selected to include available meeting evidence alongside general questions.

Parakeet adds finalized speech as the meeting progresses. Whisper transcribes after recording stops, so its current recording has no live transcript to reference. New speech becomes available to the next question; it does not change an answer already being generated.

Recording continues while you chat. Pausing or stopping recording does not discard an answer in progress, and the conversation stays attached to the saved note. You can also chat with other notes or ask general questions when live transcription is unavailable.

## What you can ask

The ask bar is useful for questions you would ask a colleague who was in the meeting:

- "What decisions were made?"
- "What did we agree to do about the pricing change?"
- "Who is responsible for the API migration?"
- "Summarize this in three bullet points."
- "What were the concerns raised about the timeline?"
- "Was there anything about [client name]?"

Questions that reference specific details from the meeting work best. Vague questions ("what happened?") return less useful answers than specific ones ("what was the outcome of the budget discussion?").

## What Steno reads

The selected context determines what Steno sends to your AI provider:

| Context | Content |
|-------|---------|
| This meeting, while recording | A snapshot of finalized speech, including earlier transcript when continuing a saved note |
| This meeting, saved note | Summary, topics, key points, action items, personal notes, and transcript |
| All notes or a folder | A bounded selection of recent saved summaries, titles, and key points |
| Shared notes | Your organisation's shared-note content |
| No meeting context | The question and recent conversation turns with this same selection, without meeting content |

Recent follow-ups are bounded and kept within the selected scope. Long contexts are truncated; local and remote Ollama budgets depend on the configured model. Older meetings may fall outside the All notes budget, so open a specific note to ask about it directly.

## Privacy

Chat follows your existing AI-provider setting. With local Ollama, questions stay on your device. A configured remote, cloud, or organisation provider receives the question and selected context. Selecting **No meeting context** excludes meeting content and conversation history from other contexts.

---

<Accordion title="Can I ask questions across multiple meetings?">
Yes. Alongside the inline ask bar for a single note, Steno supports library-wide chat across all of your meetings, so you can ask questions that span your whole meeting history.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The answers are not very accurate -- what should I try?">
Larger models produce better answers. In **Settings → AI**, switch to `qwen3.5:9b` or `gemma4:12b-it-qat`. Also check that the full transcript was saved -- if transcription failed or was skipped, the model only has the summary to work from.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I ask in a language other than English?">
Yes. The ask bar passes your question directly to the language model, which supports multilingual input. Results may vary by model and language.
</Accordion>
