Open **Cookbooks** in the sidebar to find two kinds of reusable prompt:

- A **cookbook** turns a meeting into a specific kind of note or report, such as a sales debrief, a 1:1 record or a standup digest. Pick one when you generate notes, or make it your default.
- A **recipe** is a saved prompt you run in chat, such as a weekly recap, a follow-up email or a decision log.

<Note>
Cookbooks used to be called report templates. Your existing templates and reports carry over as cookbooks.
</Note>

The Cookbooks page has three tabs: **Discover** for the built-in cookbooks, **My cookbooks** for the ones you write, and **Recipes**.

## Cookbooks

### Built-in cookbooks

| Cookbook | What it produces |
|----------|------------------|
| **Standard** | Steno's classic structured note: a summary, discussion areas, key points and action items. |
| **Product Demo** | What was shown, how well it fits, pricing, open concerns and next steps. |
| **Sales Call** | Needs, objections, budget and timeline, and the next steps agreed on a sales call. |
| **1:1** | Updates, feedback in both directions, blockers, and follow-ups with owners. |
| **Standup** | A brief digest of done, next and blocked, grouped by person or topic. |
| **Shareable summary** | A plain-language recap of key points and decisions, ready to forward. |

**Standard** is protected and can't be edited or deleted. You can edit any other built-in cookbook; click **Reset** to go back to Steno's version.

### Create a cookbook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new cookbook">
    On the Cookbooks page, click **Create**, then **New cookbook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the note">
    Enter a **Name**, choose a **Language** if you want notes in a fixed language, and write the **System Prompt** that tells the AI how to structure the note. Markdown is supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save cookbook**. It appears under **My cookbooks**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete a cookbook you made, open it and click delete. Notes already generated from it are not affected.

### Set the default

The default cookbook is used automatically for new meetings. Open a cookbook and click **Set as default**. New installs use **Standard**.

### Several reports per meeting

A meeting can have more than one report. In a meeting, use **Generate from cookbook** to create a report with a different cookbook, then switch between the note and its reports. Deleting a report doesn't affect the transcript or other reports.

## Recipes

### Built-in recipes

| Recipe | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **List recent todos** | Pulls outstanding to-dos from recent meeting notes. |
| **Coach me** | Looks for patterns and suggests areas to improve. |
| **Write weekly recap** | Summary of the week across every meeting. |
| **Blind spots** | Surfaces themes you may have missed. |
| **What do I owe people** | Your open commitments, grouped by person. |
| **Prep my next meeting** | Past context, open items and questions to raise. Finish the sentence with who the meeting is with. |
| **Draft the follow-up** | A recap email, ready to send. |
| **Decision log** | Every decision, with when and who. |
| **Write a PRD** | Turns discussions into a requirements doc. |

Built-in recipes can't be edited or deleted. To change one, copy its prompt into a new recipe.

### Run a recipe

- **From chat**: on the **Chat** page, type `/` at the start of the message box, choose a recipe, and press Return. See [Chat](/features/ask-your-meetings#use-a-recipe).
- **From Cookbooks**: on the **Recipes** tab, click **Run** on a recipe. Chat opens with the recipe's prompt filled in.

Either way the prompt fills the message box without sending, so you can edit it first. The recipe runs against the context selected in chat, which is **All notes** by default.

### Create a recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new recipe">
    On the Cookbooks page, click **Create**, then **New recipe**. Or click **New recipe** on the **Recipes** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Enter a **Name**, an optional **Description** shown under the name in the `/` menu, and the **Prompt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save recipe**. It appears under **My recipes** and in the `/` menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change or remove a recipe you made, open it from **My recipes**. Deleting a recipe doesn't affect chats that used it.

---

<Accordion title="Where are cookbooks and recipes stored?">
On this device, in Steno's data folder. Generated reports are saved alongside each meeting. Nothing is uploaded unless you use a cloud AI provider, which receives the prompt along with the meeting text.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I write a cookbook in another language?">
Yes. Set the cookbook's **Language** to pin its output language, whatever language the meeting was in. Left on auto, notes follow your summarization language setting.
</Accordion>
